Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide customised material recommendations?
Yes. We help customers select the right material based on application requirements, strength, surface finish, temperature resistance, and functional requirements. Contact us with your project details and our team will provide a tailored material recommendation.
Do you support low-volume production?
Yes. We provide both prototyping and low-volume production solutions. Whether you need a single prototype or a batch of production parts, we can support your requirements. Our services are flexible and scalable based on your needs.
Can you help with product development?
Yes. We support concept validation, prototyping, iterative design development, and manufacturing assistance. We can work with you from early concept stages through to production-ready parts, helping you choose the right technology and materials at each stage.
Do you provide technical support for 3D printing?
Yes. We assist customers with material selection, process optimisation, print settings guidance, and troubleshooting. Our technical support covers all major additive manufacturing technologies and materials that we supply and use in our services.
Which industries do you primarily serve?
We serve a wide range of industries including Automotive, Aerospace, Medical, Manufacturing, Product Design, Consumer Products, Research & Development, Educational Institutions, Tooling & Fixtures, and Industrial Engineering. If your industry isn't listed, contact us — we are happy to discuss how additive manufacturing can benefit your specific application.
What additive manufacturing technologies do you support?
We support FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling), SLA (Stereolithography), DLP (Digital Light Processing), SLS (Selective Laser Sintering), MJF (Multi Jet Fusion), and DMLS (Direct Metal Laser Sintering). We also supply materials compatible with these technologies and provide 3D printer system recommendations.
