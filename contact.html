Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats do you accept?
We accept STL, OBJ, STEP, STP, 3MF, and AMF files. If you have SolidWorks, Fusion 360, or CATIA files, we can work with native formats too. Don't have a 3D file? Our Design Assistance team can help.
How fast can you deliver?
Standard turnaround is 48–72 hours from file approval. For clients in Chennai, we offer same-day express printing for urgent requirements. Larger batch orders typically take 5–7 business days.
What is the minimum order quantity?
There is no minimum order quantity. We print single prototypes just as readily as we handle batch orders of 500+ units. Every order receives the same level of quality attention.
Do you ship pan-India and internationally?
Yes. We ship to all Indian states via BlueDart and Delhivery with tracking. For international clients, we use FedEx and DHL with full export documentation. Customs handling is managed by our logistics team.
Can you sign an NDA before reviewing my design?
Absolutely. Client confidentiality is a cornerstone of our service. We are happy to sign your NDA before any files or project details are shared. Simply mention it in your enquiry.
