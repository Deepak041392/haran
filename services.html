FDM 3D Printing

Fused Deposition Modelling (FDM) is the most versatile and widely-used additive manufacturing technology. Our industrial FDM printers extrude thermoplastic filament layer by layer to build robust, functional parts ideal for structural and mechanical applications.

SLA / Resin Printing

Stereolithography (SLA) uses a UV laser to cure liquid photopolymer resin into solid layers with exceptional surface finish and detail resolution. Our Formlabs Form 3 systems produce parts with smooth, near-injection-moulded quality straight off the print bed.

Build Volume
Up to 145×145×185 mm
Layer Resolution
0.025 – 0.1 mm
Accuracy
±0.05 mm
Turnaround
