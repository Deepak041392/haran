SLA Technology

Stereolithography (SLA)

High-precision resin printing with excellent surface finish and fine detailing. SLA uses a UV laser to cure liquid photopolymer resin into solid layers — delivering near-injection-moulded surface quality with exceptional accuracy for demanding applications.

SLS Technology

Selective Laser Sintering (SLS)

Industrial-grade nylon printing for durable and complex geometries. SLS uses a laser to sinter powdered nylon material — enabling complex internal structures, interlocking parts, and high-strength functional components without the need for support structures.

Technology
Laser Sintering (Powder)
Material

Strength

Supports

Ideal For

  • Complex geometries with internal features and channels
  • Durable functional parts for industrial applications
  • Low-volume production of end-use nylon components
  • Interlocking assemblies and living hinge designs

High-speed production technology suitable for functional and end-use parts. MJF produces parts with consistent mechanical properties, fine detail resolution, and smooth surface finish — making it ideal for medium-to-high volume production runs of complex nylon parts.

DMLS Technology

Direct Metal Laser Sintering (DMLS)

Advanced metal additive manufacturing for high-performance industrial applications. DMLS sinters fine metal powder using a high-powered laser — producing fully dense metal parts with complex geometries, internal channels, and mechanical properties comparable to machined metals.
