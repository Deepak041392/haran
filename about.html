Who We Are

Born in a Workshop,
Built for Industry

NexForge 3D was founded in 2017 by Arun Krishnamurthy and Divya Selvam — two mechanical engineers from IIT Madras who saw a critical gap in India's manufacturing ecosystem: startups and SMEs needed fast, affordable access to precision parts, but traditional manufacturing routes were too slow and too expensive for early-stage innovation.

Starting with two FDM printers in a rented workshop in Ambattur, Chennai, they delivered their first batch of prototype parts to a local automotive startup within 36 hours. That first client went on to secure Series A funding — and word spread quickly.

Today, NexForge 3D operates from a 4,000 sq ft. state-of-the-art additive manufacturing facility with a fleet of 18 industrial printers, serving clients from Kanyakumari to Kashmir — and globally through our digital delivery model.

Our Journey

Seven Years of Growth

2017

Founded in Chennai

NexForge 3D launches from a 400 sq ft workshop with 2 FDM printers and a vision to democratise precision manufacturing.

2018

First 100 Projects

Reach the 100-project milestone. Expanded to automotive and medical sectors. Added first SLA printer to serve high-detail requirements.

2020

COVID-19 Response

Pivoted to print 10,000+ face shield frames and ventilator components for Tamil Nadu hospitals during the pandemic. Recognised by MSME Ministry.

2022

New Facility & Scale

Moved to 4,000 sq ft. facility in SIDCO Industrial Estate, Ambattur. Fleet grew to 18 printers. Launched Design Assistance service.

2024

Pan-India & Global Reach

Serving clients across 14 Indian states and exporting digital manufacturing services to clients in Singapore, UAE, and the UK.
