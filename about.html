Who We Are

Born from a Passion for Manufacturing

Phalin3D Labs is an additive manufacturing solutions company focused on delivering high-quality 3D printing materials, advanced manufacturing services, and reliable 3D printing systems for industries, businesses, educational institutions, and product developers.

We specialize in engineering-grade FDM filaments, industrial SLA & DLP resins, and advanced metal and ceramic filament solutions compatible with FDM 3D printers. Our goal is to simplify and strengthen the adoption of additive manufacturing.

Along with material solutions, we provide professional 3D printing services across multiple technologies including FDM, SLA, SLS, MJF, and DMLS to support rapid prototyping, functional testing, tooling, and end-use production applications.

Our Technologies

Multi-Technology Capabilities

We work across the full spectrum of additive manufacturing technologies to deliver the right solution for your specific application.

Filament
FDM – Fused Deposition Modeling

Cost-effective and versatile technology for functional prototypes, jigs, fixtures, and engineering applications using thermoplastic materials.

Resin
SLA – Stereolithography

High-precision resin printing with excellent surface finish and fine detail resolution for dental, jewelry, and engineering applications.

Resin
DLP – Digital Light Processing

Industrial-grade resin printing for high-speed production with superior accuracy for functional and precision components.

Powder
SLS – Selective Laser Sintering

Industrial-grade nylon printing for complex geometries, durable functional parts, and end-use components without support structures.

Powder
MJF – Multi Jet Fusion

High-speed production technology suitable for functional end-use parts with consistent mechanical properties and fine feature resolution.

Metal
DMLS – Direct Metal Laser Sintering

Advanced metal additive manufacturing for high-performance industrial applications requiring exceptional strength and complex geometries.
